4. MAJOR RISKS
================================================================================

RISK 1 -- MOBILE HOME CONDITION IS THE CENTRAL UNKNOWN [HIGH]
   The listing explicitly telegraphs the mobile home may need full removal.
   Pre-1976 mobile homes in Michigan have no HUD code compliance; pre-1989
   structures carry asbestos probability in floor tiles, roofing, and
   insulation. If the home is non-structural, flooded, or infested, the only
   viable path is demolition. Without an on-site inspection you're buying blind.

RISK 2 -- ASBESTOS ABATEMENT COST SPIKE [HIGH]
   If the mobile home predates 1989, Michigan law requires an asbestos survey
   before demolition. Professional abatement for a single-wide mobile home runs
   $2,000-$8,000+ depending on material quantity. Add this to demo costs.
   Without seeing the home's year-built sticker or a prior inspection report,
   this is unbudgeted exposure.

RISK 3 -- WELL AND SEPTIC STATUS UNKNOWN [HIGH]
   Rural Pleasanton Township means well and septic. The listing makes no mention
   of the condition of either system. Possibilities:
   - Functional systems: inspection cost ~$800-$1,500, proceed
   - Failed/absent septic: $7,000-$35,000 replacement depending on soil perc
   - Failed/contaminated well: $8,000-$15,000 replacement
   - If soil won't perc conventionally (common with rolling terrain and certain
     Manistee County soils), an engineered mound system is required:
     $15,000-$35,000+

RISK 4 -- WETLAND OVERLAY [MEDIUM-HIGH]
   Pleasanton Township's zoning code has a Wetland Overlay District. Manistee
   County has extensive NWI-mapped wetlands throughout. If any portion of the
   0.87-acre parcel is in a regulated wetland, Michigan Part 303 permits are
   required before any fill, grading, or construction activity -- and permits
   can be denied. Do not close without running this parcel through EGLE's
   Wetlands Map Viewer.

RISK 5 -- ZONING FOR REPLACEMENT STRUCTURES [MEDIUM]
   Mobile/manufactured homes are not universally permitted by-right in all
   Michigan RA zones. Pleasanton Township's RA ordinance details specific
   sub-districts (RA-1, RA-2, RA-3) with different permitted uses. If you plan
   to replace the mobile home with another manufactured unit or a tiny home,
   confirm with the Manistee County Planning Dept (231-929-5000) that the
   specific sub-district allows it before purchasing. Tiny homes on wheels
   (THOWs) are typically not permitted in Michigan's rural residential zones.

RISK 6 -- SMALL LOT LIMITS HOMESTEAD USE [MEDIUM]
   0.87 acres with wooded rolling terrain leaves limited flat, usable acreage.
   After the existing structure footprint, setbacks (likely 35-50 ft from road,
   10-15 ft sides, 35 ft rear), and tree cover, the functional buildable/
   cultivatable area may be 5,000-15,000 sq ft of usable ground. Serious
   livestock, large gardens, or outbuildings on this lot will be constrained.

RISK 7 -- NATURAL GAS AVAILABILITY UNCERTAIN [LOW-MEDIUM]
   Superior Energy Company serves the Bear Lake village area. Whether a gas line
   runs to 14638 Pleasanton Hwy specifically is unconfirmed. Rural parcels
   outside the village core may have no gas service. Budget for propane delivery
   as the fallback for heating if building new.

RISK 8 -- 100-125 INCHES ANNUAL SNOWFALL [MEDIUM]
   Lake-effect snow from Lake Michigan (5 miles west) dumps heavily on this
   corridor. Foundation footings must go 42 inches minimum; expect heating,
   plowing, and winterization costs to be material. An uninsulated or poorly
   insulated mobile home will be expensive to heat.

RISK 9 -- NO FLOOD ZONE DATA CONFIRMED [LOW-MEDIUM]
   Property description as "rolling hills" is encouraging, but the FEMA flood
   zone for this specific parcel has not been confirmed. Any low-lying drainage
   easements or unnamed creek proximity need to be checked before closing.
   Flood insurance on a rural mortgage can add $800-$3,000/year.

RISK 10 -- PROPERTY CRIME AT VACANT STRUCTURES
   If left vacant while planning/permitting, the visibly distressed mobile home
   becomes a magnet for vandalism, squatting, and copper/appliance theft. Budget
   for minimal security measures or expedite the demo timeline.
