Executive Summary

This 0.87-acre wooded rural parcel in Pleasanton Township, Manistee County, Michigan is listed at $49,900 and carries a distressed mobile/manufactured home that the sellers themselves acknowledge may require removal. The land itself has genuine investment merit: electricity at the road, a county-maintained all-season road, 17 miles from Manistee city services, wooded rolling-hill terrain that is inherently unfavorable for wetlands or flooding, and a price point at the low end of comparable Pleasanton Highway investment properties. However, four critical unknowns elevate risk to Medium-High: (1) the mobile home's condition, age, and title status — if still personal property it may require costly conversion or separate negotiation; (2) well and septic condition — a failed system or non-percing soils could add $15,000–$35,000+; (3) FEMA flood zone and NWI wetlands designation — neither has been confirmed, though rolling-hills terrain strongly argues for Zone X / no wetlands; and (4) mineral rights — Manistee County has active oil/gas interest and severed subsurface rights are common in this region and must be verified at the Register of Deeds before closing. The asking price is fair if the mobile home is livable or inexpensive to demo and the environmental checks come back clean. It is a potential overpay if full demo + asbestos abatement + septic replacement is required. Recommendation: Pursue — but only after completing the 4-item critical checklist in Section 11. Do not offer without a mobile home inspection contingency.
